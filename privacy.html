CloudVault Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

CloudVault Storage Inc. ("CloudVault", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy rights. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, storage, processing, and protection of information collected when you access our website, utilize our hybrid valet storage facilities, automated staging lockers, and associated services.

1

Eligibility & Age Restriction (18+ Only)

Strict Age Requirement:

CloudVault services are strictly intended for individuals who are at least eighteen (18) years of age and legally capable of entering into binding commercial contracts. We do not knowingly market to, collect, or solicit personal information from minors under the age of 18. If we obtain knowledge that a user is under 18 years of age, their account and associated data will be terminated immediately.

2

Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to fulfill physical storage bailment, execute logistics, and process payments:

Account & Contact Identifiers

Full legal name, email address, mobile phone number, account authentication credentials, and customer role.

Physical Service & Delivery Addresses

Primary residential or commercial address, delivery instructions, apartment/unit numbers, city, state, and postal code.

Tokenized Billing Information (PCI-DSS)

Payment details are collected strictly via Stripe Elements iframes. We store only anonymized payment method tokens (pm_...), card brand, and last 4 digits. Zero raw card data is ever stored on CloudVault servers.

Inventory & Operational Telemetry

User-entered tote labels, barcode scans, warehouse slotting coordinates, single-day staging locker PINs, and time-stamped custody transfers.

3

How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Fulfillment & Storage Logistics: Storing itemized totes in our secure regional fulfillment hubs and scheduling doorstep valet deliveries.
  • Automated Staging Security: Generating time-restricted, single-day PIN codes and QR passes for self-service pickup and drop-off rooms.
  • Billing & Tax Compliance: Calculating location-based sales taxes, processing monthly subscription renewals, and generating digital receipts.
  • Fraud Prevention & Bot Defense: Employing Cloudflare Turnstile cryptographic verification and rate-limiting routines to prevent brute-force attacks and abuse.
4

Zero Data Selling & Third-Party Disclosures

✓ Our Promise: CloudVault does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data or inventory records to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

We share data exclusively with trusted technical infrastructure partners who adhere to rigorous data processing agreements:

  • • Stripe Inc.: Payment processing, fraud mitigation, and subscription billing (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified).
  • • Supabase (PostgreSQL / AWS): Encrypted database storage, authentication, and kernel-level Row Level Security isolation.
  • • Cloudflare: CDN delivery, SSL/TLS transport encryption, and anti-bot verification (Turnstile).
  • • PostHog: Application performance monitoring and workflow conversion telemetry (scrubbed of PII).
  • • Law Enforcement: Only when compelled by valid legal subpoenas, court orders, or search warrants under applicable state or federal law.
5

Your Rights (CCPA / CPRA & GDPR)

Regardless of your geographic location, CloudVault grants all users comprehensive data sovereignty rights:

Right to Know & Access You can request an export of all personal data, invoices, and storage manifests associated with your account.
Right to Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten") You may request total account erasure upon settling outstanding storage balances and retrieving your physical property.
Right to Rectification You can update your personal contact details, passwords, and delivery addresses anytime via the dashboard profile settings.
Non-Discrimination We will never deny services, charge different prices, or degrade service quality for exercising your privacy rights.
6

Security Safeguards & Data Retention

CloudVault enforces enterprise-grade physical and logical security protocols:

  • • Transport Encryption: All client-to-server and server-to-server traffic is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • • Row-Level Database Security (RLS): Multi-tenant data segregation is enforced at the database engine level, ensuring users cannot query or alter other customers' data.
  • • Session Auto-Termination: Client browser sessions automatically invalidate after 30 minutes of inactivity to protect unattended devices.
  • • Retention Limits: Operational telemetry logs are retained for 90 days. Tax and billing records are archived for 7 years in accordance with federal financial regulations.
