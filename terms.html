1

Jurisdictional Classification & Scope of Services

CloudVault operates as a commercial warehouseman under Article 7 of the Uniform Commercial Code (UCC). We combine physical logistics management, automated staging access control, dynamic volumetric billing, and distributed multi-tenant software systems.

Important Legal Distinction:

Our services involve taking physical possession, custody, and exclusive control over your itemized goods. We transport them into restricted central vaults and stage them asynchronously in automated lockers. Consequently, this relationship is governed by the statutory rules of commercial bailment under UCC Article 7, and not by state self-service storage facility acts (which govern real property leases).

We issue electronic manifests and transactional receipts that function as documents of title under UCC Article 7, detailing item descriptions, volumetric storage rates, and location logs.

Commercial Bailment & Standard of Care

As a commercial warehouseman, CloudVault assumes a statutory standard of ordinary care for all stored items. We pledge to exercise the degree of care that a reasonably careful person would exercise under similar circumstances. CloudVault does not completely exculpate itself from liability for its own negligence; however, limitations on the recoverable damages apply as detailed below.

3

Limitations of Liability & Declared Valuation

In accordance with UCC § 7-204(b), CloudVault limits the amount of damages recoverable in the event of loss or damage to your stored items.

Baseline Liability Cap:

Our standard contractual limit of liability is $50.00 per standard tote or itemized unit.

3.1 Declared Valuation Option

At the time of executing this storage agreement—or within a reasonable time after receiving your electronic warehouse receipt—you are provided the explicit opportunity to declare a higher valuation for your stored items. Selecting a higher valuation requires a proportional fee adjustment to your storage rate structure.

If you decline to declare an excess value, the baseline liability cap automatically applies. The default limitation of liability formula for your stored goods is calculated as follows:

Leffective = min(Vdeclared, Ntotes × Cbaseline)

Where Leffective represents the actionable liability limit, Vdeclared is the customer's declared valuation, Ntotes is the total active stored item count, and Cbaseline is the standard $50.00 contractual limit per item.

4

Asynchronous Custody Chain & Risk Allocation

CloudVault's physical operations involve three distinct zones: the core fulfillment vault, the transit vector, and the unstaffed staging room. Risk of loss and bailment responsibilities shift depending on the physical location and access state of the item:

  • Core Vault & Transit: CloudVault maintains exclusive physical control and bears statutory bailment liability.
  • Staging Room Lockers: Active bailment custody continues while your item sits securely in our locked automated staging bay.
  • Transfer of Custody: Legal transfer of physical possession and termination of our active bailment responsibility occurs only when you execute the single-day ephemeral PIN or QR code authentication at the physical kiosk and the kiosk door unlock signal is successfully emitted.

Time-to-Live (TTL) & Re-vaulting:

Ephemeral staging PINs are valid for a strict 12 to 24-hour window. If you fail to retrieve staged items within this TTL window, the staging lock re-engages, the PIN expires, and the item is returned to the core vault. An administrative re-vaulting fee will be assessed to your account to cover the incurred operational overhead.

  • Express Affirmative Consent: By authorizing your initial payment through an explicit, un-checked checkbox on our sign-up interface, you agree to automatic monthly renewals based on your stored volumetric capacity.

  • Unobstructed Cancellation: You maintain the right to cancel your subscription at any time. We provide a prominent, direct online cancellation mechanism within your administrative dashboard profile. Cancellation takes effect immediately without intermediate retention flows blocking your exit.
6

Default Management & Lien Enforcement

If you fail to pay your recurring subscription fees, CloudVault enforces a warehouseman’s lien on your stored goods under UCC § 7-209 and § 7-210 for charges related to storage, processing, transportation, and preservation expenses.

  • Formal Notice: Upon a payment default exceeding 30 days delinquent, we will issue a formal, itemized statement of claim to your verified electronic and physical addresses. This notice will demand payment within no less than 10 days and state our intention to sell the goods.
  • Commercially Reasonable Sale: If the claim remains unpaid, your goods will be disposed of through a commercially reasonable public or private sale in accordance with UCC regulations.
  • Proceeds: Sale proceeds will satisfy the outstanding lien. Any excess balance will be remitted to you or transferred to the state’s unclaimed property registry.
