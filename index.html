Our Cost Structure

How Our Model Passes Savings to You

Three structural advantages that allow us to charge a fraction of what traditional models cost — and pass every dollar to your rate.

High-Density Storage

Traditional facilities waste most of their floor space on hallways and access aisles. Our vertical micro-fulfillment architecture stores dramatically more per square foot — and that efficiency is reflected directly in your rate.

Unstaffed Smart Staging

No front desk. No office staff. Our keypad-secured staging rooms give you 24/7 access without the labor overhead. Those savings go straight to your monthly bill — not to a staffing budget.

Automatic Volume Tiers

The more you store, the less each tote costs. Your rate drops automatically — no negotiating, no calling to ask for a deal. The discount is built into the system.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How big is a CloudVault tote?
Standard 27-gallon heavy-duty tote (30" × 20" × 15"). Fits seasonal clothes, books, kitchen items, documents, holiday décor, and more. We provide the totes — you just fill them.
Is my stuff safe?
Absolutely. Our facilities feature 24/7 HD surveillance, and keypad-secured staging rooms. Your items are stored in a high-density warehouse that is never publicly accessible. Additionally, third party insurance is available through our integrated insurance provider for added peace of mind.
How do I get my stuff back?
Request by 6:00 PM, and we stage your selected totes overnight. The next day, drive up, enter your one-time PIN at the door, grab your items, and go. Free and included with every plan. If you want it right to your doorstep we also offer that for a fee in select locations.
What if I need more totes later?
Add totes anytime from your dashboard. Your rate automatically adjusts to the best tier — the more you store, the less each tote costs. No need to call or negotiate.
Is there a contract?
No. Month-to-month billing. Cancel anytime from your dashboard — no phone calls, no penalties.
