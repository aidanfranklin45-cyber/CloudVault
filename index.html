Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How big is a CloudVault 27-gallon tote?
Each tote is an industrial 27-gallon commercial bin measuring 30" Length × 20" Width × 15" Height with a 400-lb crush capacity. It easily holds 20–30 winter sweaters, 40+ books, full bedding sets, or seasonal holiday lights and décor. We provide clean, heavy-duty totes—you just fill them.
How do I get my stuff back?
Simply log into your dashboard, select the specific totes you want by photo, and tap "Request Staging" by 6:00 PM. Our team moves your totes to your neighborhood 24/7 keypad staging room overnight. Drive up the next day, enter your secure single-use PIN, grab your items, and go. It's completely free and unlimited. Doorstep valet delivery is also available if you prefer.
How do regional rates and volume tiers work?
Rates are dynamically determined by your local facility and drop automatically as you store more totes. When you move from Tier 1 (1–9 totes) up to Tier 4 (50+ totes), your per-tote rate drops automatically without any renegotiation.
Is there any long-term contract or cancellation fee?
No. All CloudVault subscriptions are month-to-month. You can adjust your tote count or cancel anytime directly from your dashboard without speaking to a sales rep.
Are my belongings secure and insured?
Yes. All items are vaulted in climate-monitored, restricted facilities under 24/7 CCTV surveillance. Every container is secured with a serialized tamper seal upon intake. Optional comprehensive contents insurance is available during checkout.
